## Video 1: 90 seconds

### 00:00–00:18 · The problem

It's 2 a.m.
My AI agent is working.
Not on my computer.
Somewhere in the cloud.
With my passwords. On a model I don't control.
And the bill?
Two hundred dollars. Every month.

### 00:18–00:34 · The reveal

I didn't want another assistant.
I wanted a second brain.
So, we built Loopmates.
Nineteen Mates. One computer. Mine.
Each one a brain of its own.
With a name, a voice, and a skill.

### 00:34–01:30 · The demonstration

I say it once.
"Add dark mode. Run the tests."
Planner breaks it down. Research finds the way. Code makes the changes. Ops checks the build.
Tests green.
Nice.
I wasn't even in the loop.

Something risky?
Halo asks first. I approve. Done.

What did I decide last week?
Mimo remembers.
"Shorter, please."
Said once. Remembered.

It's 11 p.m. The garage is still open.
Mimo calls my phone.
"Close it?" Yes. Lights off, too.

My models. My keys. My machine.

By morning, the work is done.
The summary's waiting.

Loopmates.
Working while I don't.

Oh, and the subscription?
There isn't one.
